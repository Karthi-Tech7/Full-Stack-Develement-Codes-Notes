South Indian Filter Coffee

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons Filter Coffee powder
  • 1/2 cup Boiling water
  • 1 cup Hot milk
  • 2 teaspoons Sugar (adjust to taste)

How to make

  1. Add coffee powder to the upper part of the filter.
  2. Pour boiling water over it and close the lid tightly.
  3. Wait 15-20 minutes for the thick decoction to drip down.
  4. Boil the milk and add sugar to it.
  5. Mix the hot milk with the decoction, pour between two tumblers to make it frothy, and serve hot!
